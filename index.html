Overview / 00

The methods people use to research well, built for AI to follow.

Professionals are increasingly asked to research a topic as part of their job: a market scan before a proposal, a regulatory check before a decision, the evidence behind a recommendation. AI makes that quick, but a quick answer is not the same as a sound one. An ordinary prompt only does what you asked for. These prompts each carry a research method, one of the things a careful researcher does that an AI would not do on its own. Use one on its own in a chat, or install the whole method as a system prompt so it runs on every task.

4 steps method-prompts for a single move one system prompt for the whole method
01
Two ways to use it / same method

One method, at whatever scale the work needs

The method is the same either way. What changes is how much of it you run at once.

A prompt, in a chat
Reach for a single method-prompt when you want one research move in the moment: chase a shaky figure, stress-test a conclusion, check a draft. Paste it into the conversation you are already in.
A system prompt, standing
Install the whole method into a project or an agent once. It then applies the full process to every research task, including the parts you would not have thought to ask for.

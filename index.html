Overview / 00

The methods people use to research well, built for AI to follow.

Professionals are increasingly asked to research a topic as part of their job: a market scan before a proposal, a regulatory check before a decision, the evidence behind a recommendation. AI makes that quick, but a quick answer is not the same as a sound one. The difference is method: the things a careful researcher does that an AI does not do on its own. AIGroundwork writes those methods as prompts, organised into four steps, and you use them at whatever scale the work needs. Reach for a single prompt in a chat to fix one thing, or install the full set as an instruction for a project or an agent, so the method runs on every task without being asked for.

The model / four steps

What you build on

It helps to see research as four steps, each resting on the one before. They are how AIGroundwork is organised, so finding your way around is also how you learn the shape of the work.

01Frame

Frame the topic

Turn a topic into a question you can actually research, before any searching starts. An AI will not question a badly framed topic. A researcher reframes it first.

Restate the questionCheck the framingPlace the effort
02Gather

Gather and verify

Collect material and work out what can be trusted, one item at a time. This is where an AI on its own falls furthest short: it reads the top few results and stops.

Spot the leadChase the detailResolve and recordKnow when to stop
03Analyse

Analyse

Produce findings that are not sitting in any single source. The core move is combination: putting two things side by side and reading what comes out.

Put two patterns side by sideRead the differencesCheck the fit
04Conclude

Conclude

Test the finding and put it in a form you can trust. Search for evidence against it, state what the checked material supports, and show what it rests on.

Look for evidence againstBuild the findingTest the draft

Overview / 00

Method prompts for desktop research, based on what people do well.

Doing research well is a craft. A careful researcher does a set of things an AI does not do on its own: framing the real question, checking what can be trusted, reading sources against each other, testing a conclusion before relying on it.

AIGroundwork writes that craft down as a framework an AI can follow, so the research you get is sound, not just quick.

The framework / five steps

Steps of the research process

It helps to see research as five steps, each resting on the one before. The how it works page explains them in full.

01Frame

Frame the topic

Where a researcher works out the real question before searching, an AI answers whatever it is handed.
02Gather

Gather and verify

Where a researcher checks what can be trusted one source at a time, an AI reads the top results and stops.
03Analyse

Analyse

Where a researcher reads two things against each other for what it reveals, an AI gives a summary.
04Conclude

Conclude

Where a researcher tests a finding and looks for what would disprove it, an AI states the most common view.
05Report

Report

Where a researcher writes the finding up so others will read it, an AI leaves it as raw output.
03
Bedrock / holds throughout

Standards that run under every step

Rank the sourcesA researcher treats a primary document differently from a marketing post. What a claim can be built on depends on where it came from, so every source is weighed before it is used.
Match effort to stakesA researcher does not check everything to the same depth. Background gets a single source; a claim the conclusion depends on gets the full chase and the search for what would disprove it.
Keep a running recordA researcher does not hold it all in their head. Every claim that matters is written down with its status and its source, so nothing is quietly asserted or quietly dropped.
RevisitA researcher goes back when something breaks. A finding that undoes earlier work sends the task back to the step it affects, rather than carrying a cracked assumption forward.
